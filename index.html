GroceryCompare.lk

A side-by-side price ledger for Sri Lanka's online grocery stores, so you know which one to open before you check out.

The website is still in the early stages of development. There may be inaccuracies in the data. The table updates everyday at around 8:00 AM. Please double check the data before making any purchase decisions.

If you find the website useful, consider supporting it by sharing it with others or making a small donation. Please support the project
